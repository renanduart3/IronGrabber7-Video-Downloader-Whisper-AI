Privacy Protocol

Last updated: February 2026

1. Overview

IronGrabber7 is a desktop application that runs entirely on your local machine. We are committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Protocol outlines what data is — and is not — collected when you use the Software.

2. No Data Collection

IronGrabber7 does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All processing — including media extraction and Whisper AI transcription — happens 100% locally on your hardware. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party service.

3. No Telemetry

The Software does not include any telemetry, analytics, tracking pixels, or usage monitoring. We have zero visibility into how, when, or where you use the Software.

4. No Account Required

Using IronGrabber7 does not require creating an account, providing an email address, or any form of registration within the application itself.

5. Cookie Bypass Feature

The Software includes a Cookie Bypass feature that reads cookies from your local browser to access authenticated content. These cookies are read locally and are never transmitted outside of your machine. They are used solely to authenticate media extraction requests directly from your device.

6. Purchase Information

Purchases are processed through third-party platforms (e.g., Gumroad, Kiwify). Any personal or payment information you provide during checkout is handled by those platforms according to their own privacy policies. We do not have access to your full payment details.

7. Local Whisper AI Processing

All AI transcription is powered by OpenAI's Whisper model running locally on your device. Audio data is never uploaded to any cloud service. Your transcriptions remain on your machine at all times.

8. Third-Party Services

The Software interacts with third-party websites (e.g., YouTube, Instagram, Twitter) solely to extract publicly available or authenticated media. We are not affiliated with any of these platforms and do not share any data with them beyond what is necessary to perform the extraction.

9. Website Analytics

This website may use minimal analytics (e.g., page views) to understand traffic. No personally identifiable information is collected through this website.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Protocol at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact
